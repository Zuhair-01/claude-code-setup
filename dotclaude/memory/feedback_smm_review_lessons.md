**Why:** an external model graded 22 drafted pieces (avg ≈90/100); every "needs rework" verdict
was for overclaim, not craft. These are the patterns worth carrying into all content drafting.

**How to apply — hold these while writing any post/reel/caption:**
- **Hook names the buyer's exact confusion, not the product.** "موزّع ولا مبدّل؟ الاسم قريب
  والاستخدام مختلف" beats "موزّع HDMI ولا مبدّل HDMI؟".
- **Every "protects / prevents / fails / lasts / breaks" verb is a fabrication risk.** Describe
  what the part *does*, never the disaster it *prevents*, unless the exact mechanism is verified.
  (A breaker interrupts the whole protected circuit — it does NOT isolate one device.)
- **Price words ("رخيص", "يوفّر بالسعر") violate zero-fabrication** when no price is supplied.
- **A spec belongs to its SKU, never its family.** "up to 200m" / "20km" must name the model.
- **No unsupported superlatives** ("أكتر سبب لرسوب فحص الشبكة", "بتحدد أداء الشبكة لسنين").
- **Carousel = saved reference; Reel = fast physical demonstration.** If the reel just
  re-narrates the carousel, it's a wasted slot.
- **Rotate the CTA-intent rung** (save / comment / WhatsApp) across the week — don't end every
  piece on "ابعتلنا مشروعك".
- **Strict cadence beats volume:** 3 feed posts/week for a real small business; stories fill
  other days, never a 4th feed post.
- **The universal test:** does the piece answer *شو المشكلة؟ / شو القرار؟ / شو المعلومات اللي
  لازم ابعتها؟* — if not, it's a product post, not a decision aid.
- **Per-pillar hashtag sets, varied per post** — never one static block on everything.

**Cycle 2 — Alwazour STUDIO's own 12-post launch batch (2026-08-30, GPT-5).** 77→~88/100.
Reference account @averlo.co. Extra lessons from this cycle:
- **The brand's headline should be its philosophy, not its service list.** "we build
  websites/apps/AI" is the evidence *under* the hook, never the hook. Alwazour's hook is
  "ما بدك تصدّقنا، بدك تفتح الرابط".
- **Borrow a reference account's energy, not its assets** — confidence, typographic
  aggression, short sentences, one-idea-per-post, a visible point of view. Never its phrases,
  layouts, or manufactured cleverness.
- **Describe posts don't work; experienced posts do.** "hello we're an agency" loses to
  "here's the evidence, go click it."
- **A stat belongs to whoever earned it** — dania's 10K/4.9★/300+ is dania's business proof,
  never the studio's performance metric. Attribute explicitly.
- Ostazi-style: if a real project is mid-beta or has no public URL, say so IN the post
  ("مرحلة تجريبية" / "ما عنده رابط عام، ما رح نعطيك رابط وهمي") — the honesty is stronger copy
  than a hedge.
