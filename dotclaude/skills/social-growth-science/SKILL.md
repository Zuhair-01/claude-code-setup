# social-growth-science

Full textbook: `Second_Brain/30-Resources/Curriculum/Product_Business_5_Pillars_Mastery.md` §5E.
Companion: `marketing-psychology` (hooks/persuasion), `video-shortform` / `youtube-shorts` (production).

## The one fact that drives everything (2026)
IG/TikTok/Shorts all optimize the same order: **retention/completion → sends (DM shares) → saves → shares → comments → likes.**
Follower relationship history barely matters now → **small accounts can go viral on content quality alone.**
Likes and follower count are lagging vanity metrics — do not optimize for them.

## Eligibility gates (check before posting)
No visible watermark (no TikTok logo on a Reel) · original (not a repost) · has audio · account in good standing · vertical 9:16 video / 4:5 carousels.

## Short-form video structure
```
0–3s   HOOK     text-on-screen (50%+ watch muted) · state payoff or tension · NO logo intro, NO "hey guys"
3–15s  DELIVER  first point immediately · visual change every 2–4s (cut/zoom/b-roll/text) to reset attention
mid    RE-HOOK  "but here's the part nobody tells you" — open a new loop before closing the old one
end    LOOP/CTA end on a line that triggers a rewatch (=2 views), OR "save this" / "send to someone who…"
```
Target ≥60% average retention on sub-30s. Pull the retention graph; fix the exact second people drop; apply to the next one.

## Hook patterns (first line / first frame)
curiosity gap · contrarian ("stop [common advice]") · specific result + timeframe · direct callout ("if you're a [X] struggling with [Y]") · open loop / numbered list · visual shock (start on the wildest frame).

## Carousels (highest-engagement format in 2026)
- Slide 1 = the hook, works as a standalone thumbnail: big text, one promise, high contrast. 80% of the outcome.
- Slide 2 = stakes / "what most people get wrong" — bridge.
- Slides 3–8 = ONE idea per slide, minimal text, consistent layout so swiping is effortless.
- Second-to-last = recap slide (the screenshot/save slide → saves are a top signal).
- Last = CTA: "follow @handle for more" + "save this" + a question for comments.
- Mechanic: each swipe = engagement; algorithm re-shows unfinished carousels → incomplete swipe is a second chance.

## Text / single posts
Insight first, context second (inverted pyramid) · one idea · white space · end on a question or "which are you?" · reply to every comment in the first hour (2026 two-way-conversation signal boosts your NEXT post to top-of-feed).

## Content system
1. Idea bank first — judge hooks/titles by "would I stop scrolling?" BEFORE producing. Title/slide-1 first, content second.
2. Pillar → micro: one long piece → 10–20 shorts + carousels + posts.
3. Volume + feedback: ~4–7 reels/wk, 2–3 carousels/wk solo. Weekly: find the overperforming hook/format/topic, do more.
4. Batch produce weekly; schedule; show up daily only for comments/DMs.
5. Steal structure not content: reverse-engineer 5 top accounts' hook patterns + cadence.

## Metrics (report in this order)
retention % · sends (DM) · saves · shares · comments · reach · follows-per-view.

## 30-day plan output
Days 1–3 niche + target person + study 5 accounts (log hooks). Days 4–7 build 30-hook bank, batch week 1 (5 reels + 2 carousels). Daily: post 1, reply to comments in hour 1, DM 3 people the content. Weekly: review retention+saves, kill bottom format, double top. Day 30: ~35 posts + real audience data (that's the asset).

## Hard rules
- Hook or nothing. A weak first 3 seconds = the whole post is dead regardless of quality after.
- No hard "follow me" CTAs mid/early — suppresses reach. Earn the follow with the content, ask softly at the end.
- Don't chase trends off-niche for views — off-target reach doesn't convert and confuses the algorithm's audience model.
- Consistency > perfection. 35 mediocre-but-improving posts beat 5 polished ones for learning what works.
